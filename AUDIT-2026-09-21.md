## 🔴 HIGH

### H1 — AI features DOWN in prod: HuggingFace credits exhausted (402) + Gemini 503s
- **Root cause:** vendor quota, not code. Live `error_log` jumped 2–4/day → 186–317/day starting 2026-09-17. Grouped prefixes (last 2d): `hf-chat moonshotai/Kimi-K2-Instruct 402` ×407, `hf-chat Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B 402` ×407, `gemini-chat 503` ×12, `hf-image sd3-medium 402 You have depleted your monthly included credits` ×2.
- **Impact:** AI chat/image/STT dead for users; every attempt burns latency then fails; error_log (1050 rows) is now quota-spam drowning real crashes.
- **Fix options:** top up HF / migrate provider / temporarily disable AI endpoints with an honest message + stop logging per-attempt quota verdicts (aggregate/sample instead).
- Code: AI chain + verdict writes `src/worker/index.ts` ~L696-920, L1532-1648, L1819, L2079.

### H2 — Logged-out account takeover via self-attested SIM (no second factor on one path)
- **Root cause:** `src/worker/index.ts:5388` — `if (!activeDevice || ... || !activeDeviceLive)` returns a fresh SESSION trusting only client-reported `sim=MATCH`. No Google proof, no OTP; sole guard is per-isolate IP rate limit 15/10 (`:5290`).
- **Impact:** anyone knowing a victim's number can claim the account after logout/session-expiry (90d) from any device. (Accounts WITH a live device are safe — they hit APPROVAL_REQUIRED.)
- **Fix options:** require Google-bound proof on this path too (recovery-style); or accept risk explicitly (breaks SIM-only reinstall UX if hardened).

### H3 — View-once media fetchable WITHOUT spending (honest-client enforcement only)
- **Root cause:** `src/worker/index.ts:9163` `GET /api/messages/:id/media` requires only conversation membership; the spend (`POST .../view`, `:9189`, race-safe conditional delete) is a separate call the viewer makes voluntarily.
- **Impact:** a modified client can fetch/save a view-once photo/video unlimited times and never call `/view`. Honest clients are fine; server never binds fetch→spend.
- **Fix options:** single-use media token minted at open time, or server-side serve-count that auto-spends after first/recipient fetch.
